Scope and payment

Revisions

Two rounds are included per deliverable. Extra rounds are billed at $50 an hour. A change in scope is quoted and approved in writing before work starts.

Projects

50% deposit before work starts and the balance on delivery, due within 7 days. The deposit is non-refundable. If a project is cancelled, work done beyond the deposit is billed at the project rate up to the date of cancellation.

Late payment

A late fee of 1.5% a month applies to an unpaid balance from the first day overdue. We send a written reminder at 7 days and pause work at 14 days until the balance is cleared. A retainer is suspended at 30 days.

Currency and fees

All invoices are in USD. Bank and transfer fees are paid by the client, so we receive the full invoiced amount. Rates are reviewed once a year, with 30 days' written notice before any increase.

What we hold ourselves to

Delays on our side

We tell you as soon as we know, with a new date, never at the deadline. If we miss a deadline we caused, you get a credit of 5% of that deliverable's fee for each week of delay, up to 20%.

Mistakes

Errors in our work are fixed at no charge and do not count as a revision round. A wrong invoice is corrected with a new due date and no late fee on the corrected amount.

Disputed charges

Tell us within 7 days of an invoice. You pay the undisputed amount on time and we review the rest within 5 working days.

Delays on your side

If feedback or assets arrive late, our deadlines move by the same number of days.

Ownership

Rights to the final work transfer to you on full payment. Until then we keep them. We may show the work in our portfolio unless we agree otherwise in writing.
